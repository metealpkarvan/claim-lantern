Accessed: 2026-10-05. Exploratory qualitative discovery, not a representative survey.

Kubacka reports checking plausible-looking references and finding nonexistent articles or mismatched DOIs. This is a historical firsthand example, not a measurement of any current model’s error rate.

## Product inference

An answer can look sourced while references fail to support its actual statements. A bookmark alone does not record which sentence was checked or why a reviewer trusted it.

Our hypothesis: A small evidence desk that separates claim, source excerpt and reviewer judgment. Attaching a URL never changes a claim to supported automatically.

The author did not request this app and has not endorsed it. Dates distinguish historical examples from current claims. No follower, revenue, effectiveness or error-rate claims are inferred.

## Why bounded

Source presence and review completion are separate metrics. Numeric and absolute-wording flags are reading prompts rather than confidence scores. An inconclusive result is valid without a source, avoiding pressure to manufacture evidence just to finish a checklist.

## Assess usefulness

Complete the workflow with your own nonsensitive example, then check whether the output preserves your intent. Record confusing steps, omissions and manual editing still needed. Acceptance checks demonstrate correct behavior, not adoption or productivity improvement.

No automatic web fetch, truth score, plagiarism detection or citation existence check. Review statuses are user assessments. A source can itself be wrong, and a quoted passage may not support the claim.
